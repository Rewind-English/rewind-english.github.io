Rewind vs. a normal camera

Why not just use the camera?

Normal camera

  • Unlock, open the app, press record
  • Too late: the moment is over
  • Can't save what already happened
  • Fills your phone with long videos

Rewind

  • One click
  • Always ready, 30 seconds behind you
  • Saves what already happened
  • Keeps only what you want
